# ACG on Table Support

Thank you for using **ACG on Table** on Apple Vision Pro.

If you experience an issue, have a question, or would like to share feedback, please contact us. We are happy to help.

## Contact Support

Email: **[cnhjr230329@outlook.com](mailto:cnhjr230329@outlook.com)**

We typically respond within **2–3 business days**.

## When Reporting an Issue

To help us investigate and resolve your issue, please include the following information in your email:

- The name and version of the app
- Your visionOS version
- A description of the issue
- The steps that led to the issue
- Screenshots or screen recordings, if applicable

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The app does not launch or respond

Make sure your Apple Vision Pro is running a compatible version of visionOS. Quit and reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart your device and try again.

If the problem persists, contact us and include your app version and visionOS version.

### How do I report a bug?

Send an email to our support address with a description of the issue and the steps required to reproduce it. Screenshots or screen recordings can also help us understand the problem.

### How can I suggest a feature?

We welcome your ideas and feedback. Send your suggestion to our support email and describe how the feature would improve your experience.

We review all feedback, but we cannot guarantee that every suggestion will be implemented.

### How do I update the app?

Open the App Store on your Apple Vision Pro and check for available updates. Keeping the app and visionOS up to date can help prevent compatibility issues.

## Privacy

Information submitted through a support request will only be used to respond to your inquiry and troubleshoot the reported issue.
